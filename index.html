About the Lorenz Attractor

The Lorenz attractor is a set of chaotic solutions to the Lorenz system, discovered by Edward Lorenz in 1963. The system is defined by three coupled differential equations:

dx/dt = σ(y - x)

dy/dt = x(ρ - z) - y

dz/dt = xy - βz

Despite being deterministic, the system exhibits chaotic behavior and extreme sensitivity to initial conditions - a hallmark of chaos theory.
